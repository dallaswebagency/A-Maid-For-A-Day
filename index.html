About Us

Independent, local and proud of it

A Maid For A Day is an independent Calgary residential cleaning company, owned and operated by Aaron Wyman. We are not a franchise. Since 1984 we have aimed for superior customer service, reliability and excellence in every home we clean.

Our staff are exceptional, and we are highly committed to the people who make that possible.
